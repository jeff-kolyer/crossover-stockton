## Priority 5 — Identify one verified unmet need

Use field visits, public records, and direct conversations to test whether a specific problem can be addressed. Feeding coverage, supplies, transportation, volunteer coordination, and veterinary access are hypotheses, not established gaps.

- [ ] Identify the specific need and evidence that verifies it.
- [ ] Document who already responds and what remains missing.
- [ ] Confirm a safe, practical action with the responsible people or organizations.
- [ ] Define how the outcome would be checked before treating the action as effective.

Do not design a new service before confirming the need.

## Working rules

- Prioritize direct participation and firsthand evidence.
- Keep planned activities, public reports, firsthand observations, verified findings, and confirmed outcomes distinct.
- Record what happened, including unanswered requests, unsuccessful attempts, and visits where no dogs were observed.
- Do not describe historical reporting areas as confirmed current hotspots.
- Publish field reports when an activity produces useful firsthand information.
- Do not treat completed activity as proof that a gap is improving.
- Avoid unnecessary website work while field activities are the priority.
