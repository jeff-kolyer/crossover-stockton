● Updated Nov 4, 2026OVERVIEW

Dogs on Stockton’s streets need care, but getting help isn’t always straightforward. Animal Services reports shelter-capacity pressure, rescue groups are responding, and the number of dogs currently on the streets remains unknown.

Reviewing 7,183 Animal Services case records and preparing to verify reported conditions firsthand. These are case records, not a count of dogs or verified street sightings.
