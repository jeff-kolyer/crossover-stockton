# Weekly dog-gap public research review

This is a **reviewed draft process**, not an automated publication schedule. Research records public evidence; Crossover field visits stay labeled as firsthand Investigation or Activity evidence.

1. Read the latest [baseline](dogs-research-baseline-2026-10.md), [`dogs-research.json`](../../src/data/dogs-research.json), the dog-related entries in [`records.json`](../../src/data/records.json), and all six entries in [`projects.json`](../../src/data/projects.json). Preserve prior IDs and review dates.
2. Check authoritative sources: Stockton Animal Services' open-data page and downloadable kennel reports; City Council agendas and project documents; primary organization reports for SSD, Hard Luck, and St. Mary's. Record the date each source was checked, including inaccessible sources.
3. Extract only comparable measurements. For each figure record species, unit, reporting interval, geographic coverage, source, evidence status, and retrieval date. Keep shelter intake, outcome, and population separate. Keep dog counts separate from all-animal counts. Preserve original category labels when their meaning is uncertain.
4. Compare new evidence with the previous baseline. Add a latest finding only for a material change: a new comparable demand or outcome figure, verified change in operational output, meaningful project milestone, corrected source interpretation, or resolved conflict. State the previous known state, current verified state, why it matters, and supporting records. No new finding is needed when nothing material changed.
5. Update structured research data and project status with source references. Funding, construction, ceremony, and operational output require distinct evidence. Set unavailable values to `null` or document them as missing; do not turn plans into completed results.
6. Produce a short change summary and an updated human-readable report. Explain whether new numbers are comparable to the earlier period. Do not add SSD encounters to shelter intake or sum shelter transfers with receiving-rescue outcomes without linked dog-level records.
7. Flag conflicting dates, category definitions, stale reports, and self-reported figures for human review. Preserve the original source claim alongside a correction; never silently overwrite historical observations.
8. Submit the data and report for review before publication. Advance `last_reviewed_at` only after the review has actually been completed, even if it found no material change. Do not alter the historical `completed_at` for the first 2026 review.
