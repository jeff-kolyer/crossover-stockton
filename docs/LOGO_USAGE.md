# Crossover identity draft

The public logo is the lowercase word `crossover.` in dark green with a rust-colored period. It appears as text in the site header, without a separate emblem. The small `c` icon is used only where a square app icon or favicon is required.

- Primary: text-only `crossover.` in the site header; `public/assets/crossover-wordmark.svg` is its export.
- Dark: `crossover-wordmark-dark.svg` on near-black backgrounds.
- Small icon: `crossover-mark.svg` for favicon and app icon, with dark and monochrome variants.
- Keep clear space around the wordmark at least the height of the `c`.
- The rust-colored period is an accent. The word remains legible without it.
- PNG mark exports at 32, 192, and 512 pixels are generated by `node scripts/render-logo.mjs` from the same geometry and colors as the SVG.

This is a local design draft for issue #61 and has not replaced the public site.
