      About & Methodology
      
    


    

      

        
What Crossover Stockton Is

        

          A pathway development platform for Stockton, CA. Instead of listing generic charities or tracking abstract problems, Crossover discovers, unblocks, and verifies concrete routes through which someone can actually help today.
        

      


      
      

        
Help Board Mode

        

          
          
        

        
Live mode stays honestly empty until a pathway is directly confirmed. Preview loads 4 example Established routes.

      


      
      

        
Four Pathway States (Non-Mandatory)

        

          

            
            
01 Exploring: Checking whether an accessible route to help exists.

          

          

            
            
02 Blocked: A real obstacle prevents helping (e.g., unresponsive inbox, 4-week waitlist, no payment route).

          

          

            
            
03 Enabling: Active intervention underway to clear a bottleneck and make a route usable.

          

          

            
            
04 Established: Verified, repeatable, and fresh (<48h). Only Established pathways appear in Help Today.
