The Core Reality

The public is not failing Stockton’s street dogs—the handoffs are breaking down. Because municipal kennels at 1575 S. Lincoln St stay packed at 128% capacity, intake shifts to appointment-only, leaving field trucks unable to pick up ~57% of reported street calls in time.

The 3 Valves That Decompress the System
  • 1. Turn Down the Faucet (+250/mo Spay/Neuter): Expand Animal Balance & ACT surgeries so unplanned litters never hit the street.
  • 2. Widen the Front Door (Field Microchip Triage): Scan found pets in the field and reunite owners on their block without using a kennel.
  • 3. Open the Back Door (+40 Foster & Pet-Friendly Beds): Expand neighbor temporary fosters, St. Mary’s Pathways pet beds, and the Spring 2027 shelter clinic.
