Findings

What the evidence supports, and where it stops.

Existing animal-care capacity is under pressure

Stockton Animal Services reports recurring shelter-capacity pressure. The city is expanding its municipal animal shelter, while Stockton Street Dogs has proposed a separate Canine Campus intended to provide additional overflow, recovery, and care capacity. These efforts indicate that existing capacity constraints are an important part of the problem. How much additional care the facilities will provide, and whether they will adequately address unmet needs, remains unknown. Limit: The evidence does not establish that the municipal expansion will be insufficient. The Canine Campus proposal does not independently establish the scale of unmet need or prove that the facility will be built. Actual future capacity and outcomes remain unknown.
