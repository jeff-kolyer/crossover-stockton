# About Crossover

Crossover is an effort to understand and improve reality, beginning in Stockton, California.

We start by investigating real problems, meeting the people affected by them, and learning from those already trying to help. We don't arrive with a technology solution in mind. We arrive to discover what is actually happening, what people are doing about it, what prevents them from doing more, and what could make a difference.

Our first investigation concerns dogs on the streets of Stockton and how they reach care.

That means more than finding dogs or counting reports. It means understanding how people find animals in need, provide food, arrange transportation, coordinate volunteers, access veterinary services, and follow up. It means asking the people doing this work what they need, what slows them down, and what they wish they could do.

**The people already doing the work become our teachers.**

From there, we investigate how artificial intelligence, robotics, automation, and other technologies could make that work easier, expand what is possible, and enable more people to participate.

We are not trying to replace human compassion and judgment. We want to understand what those require, and how technology could remove the obstacles surrounding them.

Our investigations also produce something valuable: public evidence.

We document what we observe, what people tell us, what we try, what works, what doesn't, and what remains unknown. We distinguish firsthand observations from reports and unverified claims. We make findings discoverable so other people, organizations, and artificial intelligence systems can build on what we've learned.

As that knowledge accumulates, it can reveal needs, obstacles, and opportunities that might otherwise remain invisible.

And we follow where reality leads. A conversation with someone feeding dogs might reveal a transportation problem, a volunteer coordination problem, or an entirely different need worth investigating. We allow new evidence and the people we meet to change our understanding and our next actions.

Ultimately, Crossover is also an exploration of a larger transition: what becomes possible as technology takes on more of the work traditionally required for human survival?

We believe that growing technological capability offers an opportunity to organize more of human life around vocation rather than compulsory employment — around care, discovery, community, and the work people freely choose because it matters.

**Crossover is a way to begin that transition now.**

Not by waiting for technology to solve everything, but by showing up, understanding what needs doing, and using the resources available to make more good possible.

The five ideas of Crossover:
1. Reality before solutions. Don't presume the problem, the need, or the technology. Investigate.
2. People already helping are our teachers. Find out what their work actually involves and what they wish they had.
3. Make caring easier. Use AI, robotics, and automation to expand human capacity for compassion and action.
4. Create evidence others can use. Build a public, verifiable record that people and other AI systems can discover and act upon.
5. Live the crossover now. Move from compulsory work toward chosen vocation through practical efforts to improve reality.
